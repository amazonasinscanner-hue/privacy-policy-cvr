Effective Date: April 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Amazon CVR Tool handles user information. We are committed to transparency and data privacy.

1. No Data Collection

The Amazon CVR Tool does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data. All calculations for Conversion Rate (CVR) are performed locally within your browser session.

2. Use of Permissions

  • activeTab: Used only to interact with your current Amazon Advertising tab when the extension is activated.
  • scripting: Used to display the calculation helper and CVR badges on the page.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or trade any user information with third parties. Your data never leaves your computer.
